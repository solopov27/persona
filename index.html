An audio plugin for composers

Your piano part, played like a real pianist would play it.

PERSONA takes the MIDI piano part you wrote and performs it in the style of a pianist you choose. Your notes stay exactly your notes. Only the feel changes. Piano is where it starts.

How it works

  1. Write your part

    Compose your piano part in MIDI, the way you already do in Logic or your usual music software.

  2. Pick a persona

    Choose a persona: a playing style learned from a pianist who agreed to take part.

  3. Hear it performed

    Get back a performance that sounds like that pianist playing it. Same notes, real touch.

Why it matters

Most of us spend hours nudging velocities and timing so a sampled piano stops sounding like a machine. Built-in "humanize" mostly adds random wobble. A pianist doesn't play randomly.

Mockups that sound played

Turn a sketch into a performance you can send to a director, a client or a game team, without days of hand-editing.

One voice across a whole score

Use the same persona on every cue, so the piano keeps a consistent character from the first scene to the last.

The vision

A real performer's feel, for every part you write.

Imagine writing a scene and hearing it played as if a real pianist, violinist, guitarist or singer performed it, in one consistent style across a whole score or game level. PERSONA starts with piano because it is the most common mockup instrument and the hardest to make sound human. The goal is a performer's feel for any instrument and any ensemble.

  1. PianoVersion 1. The one we are building now.
  2. More instrumentsAdded one at a time, each done properly.
  3. VoicesOnly from singers who agree to it.
  4. Full ensemblesPlayers that sound like they are listening to each other.
  5. Film and game workflowsSketch a temp score in your music software and hear a realistic performance before any musician is hired. Keep the same "performer" across a whole film or game.

Version 1 is piano only. Everything after it is the roadmap, not a promise of dates. Every performer persona, on any instrument, will come from artists who agreed and signed off.

Rights-first

PERSONA is built so pianists are partners, not raw material.

  • Consent first. Every persona comes from a pianist who agreed to it and signed off.
  • No hidden copying. We don't quietly imitate famous artists or train on recordings we have no right to use.
  • Your music stays yours. It runs offline on your computer, and the notes you wrote are never changed.
